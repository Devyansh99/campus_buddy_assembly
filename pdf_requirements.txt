Welcometothe"OP-CODE: Your Personalized Student Life Manager" project!! Inthisassignment,youwillcreate
a comprehensive student life management system in MIPS assembly language. In this assignment you’re building
a tool that you can actually use in your daily campus life to manage your schedule, track academic progress, and
optimize your time. Here, every student’s program will be completely personalized based on their student ID and
actual academic data. No two submissions should be same !!
Core Requirements
1. Personalized Student Profile System
• Input: Your student ID (hardcoded in the program)
• Output: Personalized profile showing your academic standing
• Implementation: Use your ID to generate unique academic patterns
2. Smart Daily Schedule Manager
• Display YOUR actual class schedule for the current day
• Show free time slots between classes
• Calculate time remaining until next class
• Suggest whether you should go to your hostel room, library or the canteen.
3. Academic Progress Tracker
• Calculate required SGPA to reach your target CGPA
• Track credit completion progress
• Visualize progress toward graduation
• ImplementtherequiredSGPAcalculationusingPlaksha’sofficialCGPA/SGPAcalculationrules(useyourcurrent
CGPA, completed credits, current semester credits, and target CGPA accordingly)
4. Attendance Planning System
• For each subject, calculate classes needed to reach attendance cutoff
• Use university’s/course official attendance policy to determine how many additional classes are required to meet
the attendance percentage cutoff
• Show current attendance status and alerts
5. Activity Recommendation Engine
• Suggest study sessions, games, or club activities during free slots
• Recommendations should be personalized based on your schedule gaps
• Consider time of day and duration for appropriate suggestions
